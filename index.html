01 — About

From messy data to
working systems.

I like taking ambiguous data problems and shipping something a real user can act on — whether that's a dashboard, a pipeline, or a research prototype.

I'm a Master's student in Computer Science (Data Science) at Lovely Professional University, building on a Bachelor's in the same field. My work sits at the intersection of data analytics, data engineering, and machine learning — I enjoy turning raw, unstructured data into dashboards, pipelines, and decisions people actually use.

Alongside applied dashboard work, I research further out — simulating black hole dynamics with efficient data structures, and prototyping neurosymbolic, world-model-based architectures for embodied AI. I care about work that's rigorous enough to publish and practical enough to ship.
